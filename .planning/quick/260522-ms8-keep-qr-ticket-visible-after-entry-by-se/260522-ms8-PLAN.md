# Quick Task 260522-ms8: Keep QR ticket visible after entry

## Goal

Separate QR credential validity from admission state so the customer can keep showing the QR ticket after entry, while the reservation detail clearly shows that entry has already been processed.

## Tasks

1. Split customer QR payload status from admission status.
   - Files: `packages/shared/src/types/booking.types.ts`, `packages/shared/src/schemas/booking.schema.ts`, `apps/api/src/modules/ticket/qr-ticket.service.ts`
   - Action: Add entry/admission fields to `QrTicket`, keep active credentials able to return token/JTI after entry, and treat `usedAt` as entry state instead of QR invalidation.
   - Verify: Used/entered ticket snapshots still include a QR token and expose `entryStatus = ENTERED`.
   - Done: Customer QR remains visible after entry through an active credential plus separate `entryStatus`.

2. Stop entry consume from invalidating the QR credential.
   - Files: `apps/api/src/modules/field-operations/field-check-in.service.ts`
   - Action: On entry consume, set `usedAt` but keep `tickets.status = active`.
   - Verify: Duplicate entry scans still deny through `usedAt`/scan-event checks, while future operation scans can continue to authenticate the same QR credential.
   - Done: Entry processing no longer converts the ticket credential to a one-use credential.

3. Update reservation detail UI and tests.
   - Files: `apps/web/components/reservation/reservation-detail.tsx`, related tests
   - Action: Always render QR for active credentials, and show a separate entry-completed notice below the QR when entry is processed.
   - Verify: Targeted API/shared/web tests cover active QR with entered state.
   - Done: Users see the QR plus a distinct entry completion state.
