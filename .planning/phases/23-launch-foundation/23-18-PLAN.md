<diagnosis>
`next-intl` 4.11.0 `createMiddleware(routing)` sets `X-NEXT-INTL-LOCALE` and rewrites internal requests to `/{locale}{pathname}`. With `localePrefix: 'as-needed'`, default Korean `/` rewrites to `/ko`, and `/auth` rewrites to `/ko/auth`. This project currently uses a flat App Router tree (`apps/web/app/page.tsx`, `apps/web/app/auth/page.tsx`, etc.) and has no `apps/web/app/[locale]/...` segment, so those rewritten paths render `not-found`.

Do not migrate the whole app into `[locale]` in this gap closure unless the flat-route strategy proves impossible. A full route-tree move has a larger blast radius and can break admin/API/booking layouts. Prefer a narrow custom proxy that:
- preserves `/` and unprefixed Korean paths as the actual internal routes,
- maps `/en/...`, `/th/...`, `/zh-CN/...`, and `/zh-TW/...` to the existing flat internal path,
- sets `X-NEXT-INTL-LOCALE` so `getLocale()` and `getMessages()` keep working,
- preserves `NEXT_LOCALE` and `locale-suggestion` cookie behavior,
- leaves `/api`, `/_next`, static assets, and `/admin` behavior safe.
</diagnosis>
</context>
